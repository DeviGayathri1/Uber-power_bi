# Uber Trip Analysis Dashboard  

## 📌 Project Overview  
This project focuses on analyzing Uber trip data using **Power BI**. The goal is to identify trends, patterns, and insights that can support decision-making for business growth.  

As a **Business Analyst**, this project demonstrates how data visualization and reporting can be used to:  
- Understand customer demand  
- Optimize trip operations  
- Improve resource allocation  
- Support business strategy with data-driven insights  

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives  
1. Analyze trip demand across different time periods.  
2. Identify peak hours and locations for better driver allocation.  
3. Study patterns in trip frequency to optimize pricing models.  
4. Provide management with actionable dashboards for faster decision-making.  

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies  
- **Power BI**: For building interactive dashboards  
- **Data Cleaning & Transformation**: Using Power Query  
- **Data Modeling**: Relationships and measures in DAX  

---

## 📊 Dashboard Features  
- **Trip Volume Analysis** – Trips by date, time, and day of the week.  
- **Peak Hour Analysis** – Identification of high-demand time slots.  
- **Location Insights** – Pickup and drop-off hotspots.  
- **Trend Analysis** – Seasonal or monthly patterns.  

---

## 👩‍💼 Business Analyst Contribution  
As a Business Analyst, my role involved:  
- Gathering business requirements (decision-making needs).  
- Identifying KPIs (e.g., number of trips, peak demand hours, location density).  
- Translating raw data into visual insights.  
- Communicating findings to stakeholders in a clear, actionable format.  

---

## 📈 Key Insights (Sample)  
- Most Uber trips occur during **weekday mornings and evenings**.  
- High demand is concentrated around **business districts**.  
- Seasonal demand variations suggest promotional opportunities.  

---

2. Open in **Power BI Desktop**.  
3. Explore dashboards and interact with slicers/filters.
